We standardize on IKO, and the choice we walk almost every homeowner through is
the same one: **IKO Dynasty or IKO Nordic?** They share most of their DNA — the
real fork is impact resistance and what it can do for your insurance. Here's the
comparison straight from IKO's published specs, no embellishment.

## What they share

Both are premium laminated (architectural) shingles built around the same core
engineering, so a lot of the spec sheet is identical:

- **The ArmourZone.** Both use IKO's reinforced 1¼-inch nailing band — a wider,
  tougher target that resists the wind uplift that peels ordinary shingles.
- **130-mph limited high-wind warranty** when installed to IKO's requirements.
- **Limited Lifetime** product warranty, plus **Iron Clad Protection** — the
  first 15 years are full, non-prorated coverage for manufacturing defects.
- **10-year algae-resistance warranty** against the blue-green streaking that
  Gulf-Coast humidity causes — the same _Gloeocapsa magma_ ARMA calls the most
  prevalent cause of roof discoloration in the Gulf states.
- **Class A fire rating** (ASTM E108/UL 790) over an approved deck.

So on wind, warranty length, algae, and fire, it's a wash. The decision comes
down to one spec.

## The real difference: impact class

**IKO Dynasty is UL 2218 Class 3.** **IKO Nordic is FM 4473 / UL 2218 Class 4 —
the highest impact rating there is.** Nordic earns it because its mat is
**polymer-modified (SBS) asphalt**: the shingle flexes like a shock absorber
instead of cracking, which is exactly what you want under Montgomery County hail.

One piece of honesty IKO states plainly, and so do we: **the impact rating is a
lab classification, not a guarantee against hail — and hail damage isn't covered
by the shingle warranty.** Its practical value is that a Class 4 roof can help you
qualify for an **insurance premium discount** where your carrier offers one. The
Texas Department of Insurance notes Class 4 coverings receive the highest
available credit, but every company sets its own amount, so ask yours what a
Class 4 roof would save you each year — that number is usually what decides it.

## Colors

Dynasty comes in the widest IKO palette — 15 published colors, from Granite Black
and Sentinel Slate to Cornerstone Weatherwood — so it's easy to find a muted shade
your village design-review committee will approve. Nordic offers a focused set of
six (Olde Style Weatherwood, Granite Black, Shadow Brown, Summit Grey, Glacier,
and Driftshake). If a very specific color is driving the decision, Dynasty gives
you more room; if you're set on maximum impact protection, Nordic's six are all
Standards-friendly.

| ---------------- | ----------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Type             | Laminated architectural | Laminated, polymer-modified (SBS) |

## So which one?

1. **Do the insurance math first.** Call your carrier and ask what a Class 4 roof
   credits you per year. If the annual discount is meaningful, Nordic often pays
   for its upgrade over the life of the roof — see
   [our Class 4 breakdown](/blog/class-4-impact-resistant-shingles-texas).
2. **Weigh your hail exposure.** Heavily hail-hit stretch of Montgomery County?
   Nordic's shock-absorbing mat is built for it.
3. **Then look at color.** If a specific shade matters most and hail/insurance are
   secondary, Dynasty's wider palette wins.
