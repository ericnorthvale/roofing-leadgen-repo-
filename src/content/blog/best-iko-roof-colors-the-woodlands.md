Color is the decision homeowners agonize over most — and in The Woodlands, it's
the one with a rulebook. Here's how to choose an IKO color you'll love that also
clears your village's design review, plus the shades we're asked for most.

## First, the one hard constraint

The Woodlands Township's Residential Development Standards require your roof color
to be **muted, without a color pattern, and harmonizing with the natural
landscape** — and every re-roof needs prior written approval from your village's
Residential Design Review Committee. In practice that rules out anything loud and
favors greys, browns, weathered-wood blends, and blacks. The good news: almost
every IKO color we install is designed to sit in exactly that muted range. (We
prepare and file the RDRC submittal for you as part of every
[Woodlands roof replacement](/the-woodlands/roof-replacement).)

## The most-requested colors here

Across Dynasty (our standard line) and Nordic (the Class 4 upgrade), a handful of
colors do most of the work in this market:

- **Granite Black** — the safe, sharp default. A near-black that frames white
  trim and reads clean on brick, stone, and modern exteriors alike. Available on
  both Dynasty and Nordic.
- **Summit Grey** — a true mid-grey for contemporary and transitional homes,
  without going stark. Also on both lines.
- **Shadow Brown** — warm dark brown that ties into tan brick and stone; a
  Woodlands staple under heavy tree cover.
- **Olde Style Weatherwood** — a blended brown/grey/amber wood-tone that hides
  the debris shadows shaded lots collect.
- **Glacier** and **Driftshake** — lighter, cooler options for a brighter,
  modern or farmhouse look, popular with white brick and light siding.

Dynasty offers a wider palette (fifteen colors, including Sentinel Slate,
Cornerstone Weatherwood, and Brownstone), while Nordic focuses on six — so if a
very specific shade is driving your decision, Dynasty gives you more room.

## Match the roof to the house, not the other way around

A few rules of thumb we use at the kitchen table:

- **White or light brick / stucco:** almost anything works — Granite Black and
  Glacier are the crowd favorites for contrast.
- **Red brick:** lean warm-neutral — Weatherwood blends and Granite Black keep it
  from clashing.
- **Tan / brown brick or stone:** Shadow Brown, Brownstone, and the Weatherwoods
  pull the whole exterior together.
- **Heavily shaded, wooded lot:** blended tones (Weatherwood) hide the leaf-shadow
  and debris streaking that solid colors show.

## Does color change performance — or price?

Two honest answers. **Price:** no — within a line, every color costs the same;
the price difference is Dynasty vs. the Class 4 Nordic, not the color. **Heat:** a
darker roof absorbs more heat, but attic temperature is driven far more by
ventilation than color, so we size intake and exhaust on every job. If staying
cooler is a priority, a lighter shade like Glacier or Frostone Grey helps at the
margins.
